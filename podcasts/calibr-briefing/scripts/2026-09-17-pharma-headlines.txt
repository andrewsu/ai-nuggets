Second, Novartis killed an A-L-S program. The company has discontinued V-H-B nine thirty-seven, also called lifonebart, a stabiliser and activator of T-R-E-M two, the microglial receptor that has become the field's main lever on the innate immune side of neurodegeneration. The Phase two ASTRALS trial randomised two hundred fifty-one people with early-stage disease and missed both the primary and the secondary endpoints. The primary was a composite of ventilator-free survival and change on the A-L-S Functional Rating Scale, Revised. The secondaries were decline in respiratory function and neurofilament light, the standard axonal-damage biomarker. The discontinuation surfaced in an August twenty-fifth letter to the European organisation for professionals and people with A-L-S and Novartis confirmed it Wednesday morning; detailed results go to the international A-L-S and motor neurone disease symposium in December. The T-R-E-M two hypothesis is not dead — the same molecule's Phase two in Alzheimer's is still enrolling, and Alzheimer's is where the human genetics for that receptor actually point. But for Novartis this is the fourth asset stumble in under a month, after pelacarsen missing on cardiovascular risk on September fourth, del-desiran failing in myotonic dystrophy type one, and the suspension of the rapcabtagene C-A-R-T studies following three patient deaths. Shares fell thirteen percent last week and the board is taking questions about capital deployment. Separately, on Tuesday Axoltis also missed a Phase two primary in A-L-S with N-X two-ten-c, so it was a bad week for the indication generally.

Fourth, the cleanest efficacy signal of the day came from Huntington's disease. Skyhawk Therapeutics released final fifteen-month results for S-K-Y oh-five-fifteen, a once-daily oral R-N-A splicing modulator. Patients on drug improved by an average of zero point nine four points on the composite functional, cognitive and motor scale, against a decline of zero point six five in an untreated natural-history comparison group — a one point five nine point difference, where roughly one point two points is the commonly cited threshold for clinically meaningful. The separation was statistically significant from the nine-month mark onward and held at every checkpoint. No serious adverse events. The mechanism is the interesting part: the drug does not only cut production of mutant huntingtin, which it lowered by more than sixty percent at the higher dose — it also knocks down P-M-S-one, a mismatch-repair gene whose activity drives somatic expansion of the C-A-G repeat. So it attacks the protein and the process that makes the mutation worse with age. P-M-S-one messenger R-N-A fell a consistent twenty-five percent. The caveat is the comparator: this is an external natural-history control, not randomised placebo. That is what the FALCON program is for — one trial has completed enrolment with one hundred forty-four patients in Australia and New Zealand, and a worldwide study is still recruiting toward about six hundred. Skyhawk is Fidelity-backed and has stayed private since a one hundred thirty-three million dollar round in twenty twenty-one.

Fifth, a financing with a clear thesis. Sling Therapeutics raised a one hundred twenty-three million dollar Series C led by Forbion, with T-P-G Life Sciences Innovations and Sectoral, and started a global Phase three called Orbit for linsitinib in thyroid eye disease. Linsitinib blocks the I-G-F-one receptor — the same target as Amgen's Tepezza and Viridian's Lumvoa — but it is an oral small molecule taken twice daily rather than an infusion or injection. Sling licensed it from Astellas in twenty twenty-two after more than a dozen cancer trials, which is why it moved quickly; oral I-G-F-one receptor inhibitors largely failed in oncology, and chief executive Ryan Zeidan's argument is that the target is far better validated in thyroid eye disease and that linsitinib hits it more selectively than the cancer-era compounds did. An earlier late-stage trial showed a statistically significant fifty-two percent response rate at twenty-four weeks with no drug-related hearing loss or tinnitus, which matters because ototoxicity is the signature liability of the approved antibodies. Total raised since twenty twenty-two is two hundred million.

Laromestrocel is an allogeneic mesenchymal stromal cell product from the bone marrow of young healthy donors, given during the second-stage palliative surgery; the primary endpoint, change in right ventricular ejection fraction at twelve months, came in at a least-squares mean difference of minus zero point seven points, p equals zero point eight three three six, in forty infants.
