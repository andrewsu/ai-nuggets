# Sac-TMT — TROP2 ADC wins overall survival, not just PFS, in post-platinum post-IO endometrial cancer (TroFuse-005, ESMO 2026 Presidential Symposium LBA11)

Paper link: https://www.biospace.com/press-releases/sacituzumab-tirumotecan-sac-tmt-reduced-the-risk-of-death-by-32-and-the-risk-of-disease-progression-or-death-by-41-compared-to-chemotherapy-in-certain-patients-with-advanced-or-recurrent-endometrial-cancer

Trial: NCT06132958. Sponsor: Merck (MSD), asset licensed from Kelun-Biotech. Global lead investigator Domenica Lorusso, Humanitas, Milan.

## Script

Good morning, Justice. The closing weekend of E-S-M-O in Madrid delivered the readout that settles a long-running argument about the Trop-two antibody-drug conjugate class, and it came out of gynecologic oncology rather than lung. Merck presented the Phase three TroFuse-oh-oh-five trial of sacituzumab tirumotecan — sac-T-M-T, the Kelun-Biotech-originated Trop-two A-D-C — in a Presidential Symposium slot yesterday evening. In women with advanced or recurrent endometrial cancer who had already had platinum chemotherapy and a P-D-one or P-D-L-one inhibitor, sac-T-M-T beat treatment of physician's choice — doxorubicin or paclitaxel — on both of its dual primary endpoints at a pre-specified interim. Overall survival, hazard ratio zero-point-six-eight, median nineteen-point-oh months against twelve-point-one, p less than oh-point-oh-oh-oh-one. Progression-free survival by blinded independent central review, hazard ratio zero-point-five-nine, seven-point-two months against three-point-nine, also p less than oh-point-oh-oh-oh-one. Seven hundred seventy-six patients. The F-D-A has already accepted a B-L-A under priority review with a target action date of June fifteenth, twenty-twenty-seven.

Here is why that first hazard ratio is the whole story. The Trop-two A-D-C class has spent three years being very good at progression-free survival and conspicuously bad at overall survival. The canonical example is TROPION-Lung-oh-one: datopotamab deruxtecan met its P-F-S co-primary against docetaxel, then returned an overall survival hazard ratio of zero-point-nine-four with a p-value of zero-point-five-three. Twelve-point-nine months against eleven-point-eight. Even the pre-specified non-squamous subgroup, the one everybody retreated to, had a confidence interval crossing one. Trodelvy's EVOKE-oh-three in front-line lung was stopped. The skeptical read on the whole class became that Trop-two A-D-Cs reliably shrink tumors, reliably delay the scan that declares progression, and then hand the patient back to the natural history of their disease. TroFuse-oh-oh-five is the first global Phase three in which a Trop-two A-D-C wins both endpoints against chemotherapy with this kind of separation. That is a class-level result dressed up as a gynecologic one.

Now the detail I would want you to sit with, because it is the unusual shape in this dataset. The proportional effect is larger on progression-free survival — hazard ratio zero-point-five-nine versus zero-point-six-eight. Nothing surprising there; that is the normal ordering. But look at the absolute gaps. The P-F-S medians are separated by three-point-three months. The overall survival medians are separated by six-point-nine months. The survival benefit in absolute time is more than double the progression-free benefit. Usually it runs the other way, because post-progression therapy in the control arm erodes whatever advantage you built. Here the curves keep separating after the point where the A-D-C has formally stopped working. That is what durable disease control looks like, as opposed to a deep but temporary response, and it is consistent with the duration-of-response numbers: eleven-point-one months against six-point-nine.

Which brings up something worth noticing about where the benefit is not coming from. Objective response rate was thirty-five-point-one percent against seventeen-point-three — a doubling, which is the headline Merck is running with. But complete responses were six-point-one percent against four-point-five percent. The entire response advantage is partial responses. So this is not a drug that is eradicating disease more often than doxorubicin; it is a drug that shrinks more tumors, shrinks them for longer, and that longer holds up in the survival curve. For your A-D-C programs that is the more useful mental model than the response-rate number — the asset is winning on duration, not on depth.

On tolerability, the numbers do the same thing we saw in the melanoma H-D-A-C readout on Friday, and it is becoming a reflex worth having. Grade three or higher treatment-related adverse events were fifty-four-point-four percent on sac-T-M-T against forty-one-point-four percent on chemotherapy — so the A-D-C is, straightforwardly, the more toxic arm by incidence. Anemia at twenty-four percent, neutropenia at twenty percent, stomatitis at ten percent. Classic Trop-two normal-epithelium distribution plus a topoisomerase-one payload. And yet discontinuation for toxicity was three-point-one percent on sac-T-M-T against four-point-nine percent on chemotherapy. Lower. When grade three events go up and discontinuations go down, you are looking at toxicity that is dose-modifiable rather than organ-threatening — cytopenias you hold a dose for, not pneumonitis or an irreversible endocrinopathy. Treatment-related deaths were three patients in each arm.

Three things to hold loosely. First, this is an interim analysis at fourteen months of median follow-up, so the survival estimate will move. Second, the comparator is doxorubicin or paclitaxel, which is a genuinely weak regimen — but it is also the actual standard after platinum and checkpoint blockade, so the trial is not stacking the deck so much as describing a setting with nothing in it. Note also that the protocol excluded women relapsing more than twelve months after curative-intent platinum, which strips out the easiest-to-treat patients rather than enriching for them. Third, and this is the real gap, the release carries no biomarker breakdown — no mismatch-repair-status split, no Trop-two expression data, no breakdown by whether the prior checkpoint inhibitor was given with chemotherapy or sequentially. In a population where frontline dostarlimab and pembrolizumab combinations have made essentially every second-line patient a post-I-O patient, the mismatch-repair subgroup is exactly what tells you whether this is a broad second-line standard or a drug that is doing most of its work in one biology. The data went to the Lancet simultaneously, so that table exists — go read it.

The strategic footnote, since it is relevant to how you read the rest of this asset's output. Merck is running eighteen global Phase threes on sac-T-M-T, ten of them in women's cancers, more than fifteen thousand patients. That is breadth as a development strategy, and the Trop-two field's problem has never been finding a tumor where the target is expressed. Endometrial is the one where the survival curve finally cooperated. That is the brief.
