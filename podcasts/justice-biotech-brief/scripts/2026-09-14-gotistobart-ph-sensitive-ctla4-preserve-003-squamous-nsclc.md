# Gotistobart — a pH-sensitive anti-CTLA-4 nearly doubles survival in post-immunotherapy squamous lung cancer

Paper link: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-026-04323-8

Paper link: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/09/14/3360734/0/en/biontech-and-oncoc4-present-updated-data-showing-gotistobart-nearly-doubled-median-overall-survival-versus-standard-of-care-chemotherapy-in-previously-treated-squamous-non-small-ce.html

## Script

This afternoon in Seoul, in a mini-oral slot rather than a plenary, BioNTech and OncoC4 put up updated survival data for gotistobart in squamous lung cancer that has already progressed on a checkpoint inhibitor and on platinum chemotherapy. Eighteen and a half months median overall survival against ten months for docetaxel. Hazard ratio zero point five six. Forty-five patients against forty-two.

Start with the population, because it is the whole reason to care. Metastatic squamous non-small cell lung cancer, no actionable driver, progressed after a P-D-one or P-D-L-one inhibitor and after platinum. What these people get is docetaxel, with or without ramucirumab, and they live somewhere between eight and nine point four months. That standard of care has not meaningfully moved in twenty years. Datopotamab deruxtecan came at it and missed on survival. So doubling the median here is not an incremental win in a crowded space. There is no crowd.

The molecule is the interesting part, and the mechanism is worth getting right because it inverts something most of us assume. Gotistobart is an anti-C-T-L-A-four antibody, and C-T-L-A-four is a receptor that lives mostly inside the cell, cycling constantly between intracellular vesicles and the plasma membrane. When ipilimumab binds surface C-T-L-A-four, the complex gets internalized and routed to the lysosome, and both the receptor and the antibody are destroyed. Gotistobart was engineered so that its binding is pH-sensitive: in the acidic endosome it lets go, the receptor recycles back to the surface, and the antibody comes back out too.

Two consequences fall out of that, and they point in opposite directions from each other. In the tumor, where regulatory T cells carry the highest surface C-T-L-A-four density in the body, preserving that density is exactly what an Fc-mediated depleting antibody needs, because you cannot kill a cell through a target that your own antibody has already stripped off it. In the periphery, where C-T-L-A-four is doing the tolerance work that keeps you from developing colitis and hypophysitis, you would rather not deplete the receptor at all. Same pH switch, both problems.

That is the thesis. Now the data, and I want to hold the thesis and the data apart, because they are not the same claim.

This was stage one of PRESERVE-zero-zero-three, and stage one is explicitly non-pivotal and explicitly not powered to show efficacy. Its job was dose confirmation. At a median follow-up of twenty-five point four months, eighteen point five months versus ten, hazard ratio zero point five six, nominal p value zero point zero two nine five. The word nominal is load-bearing — this is not formal hypothesis testing. And there is a March cut of the same cohort published in Nature Medicine where median follow-up was fourteen point five months, median survival on gotistobart had not been reached, and the hazard ratio was zero point four six with a nominal p of zero point zero one zero two. So with eleven more months of follow-up the hazard ratio drifted from zero point four six to zero point five six. The curves converged a little. Still a large effect, but the first number was the flattering one.

Here is what I think is the real finding, and it is in the secondary endpoints rather than the headline. Median progression-free survival was two point four months on gotistobart and two point six months on docetaxel. That is not a typo and it is not rounding. The progression-free hazard ratio was zero point six nine with a confidence interval that crosses one. Nearly half the gotistobart patients — forty-eight point nine percent — had progressive disease as their best response. And yet: confirmed response rate twenty percent against four point eight percent, median duration of response eleven months against three point eight, and the twelve-month progression-free rate was twenty-five point two percent on gotistobart against exactly zero percent on docetaxel.

So this drug does nothing whatsoever for the median patient and a great deal for about one in five. It is not shifting a distribution, it is building a tail. That is the classic C-T-L-A-four signature and it has always been the hard part about developing these molecules, because a tail is a small number of patients carrying the entire survival curve, and small numbers of patients are exactly what small exploratory cohorts are bad at estimating.

Which brings me to the part of this story that I do not think the press release is being straight about.

Stage one enrolled two hundred seventeen patients, not eighty-seven. Ninety-one were squamous. One hundred twenty-six — the larger half — were non-squamous. And in the non-squamous patients, median survival, twelve-month survival and median progression-free survival were all numerically better in the docetaxel arm. Worse than chemotherapy. A data monitoring committee ran a futility analysis by histology in September of 2024 and recommended restricting the trial to squamous; patients with non-squamous tumors already on gotistobart were encouraged to withdraw. The FDA then put a partial clinical hold on the trial in October of 2024 over precisely that histology discrepancy and lifted it in December with squamous-only enrollment.

So the number that led the press release today is the subgroup that generated the enrichment hypothesis, reported back to us as confirmation of that hypothesis, at a sample size of eighty-seven, with a nominal p-value, from a stage that says on its face that it was not designed to demonstrate efficacy. That is not evidence that squamous is the right population. It is the observation that made someone choose squamous.

The mechanistic story offered for the split is coherent and also obviously retrofitted. Squamous tumors carry higher mutational burden and a more inflamed microenvironment, and more than half of squamous samples have P-T-E-N loss, which is associated with heavier regulatory T cell infiltration — so more Tregs to deplete, bigger effect from a Treg-depleting antibody. I believe every individual clause of that. I also note it was written after the split was seen, and that the same argument would have predicted ipilimumab monotherapy works in squamous lung cancer, which it broadly did not.

Two things keep me from dismissing it. Histology was a pre-specified stratification factor at randomization, so within the squamous cohort the arms really are balanced — this is a randomized comparison, not a data-dredged subgroup. And the primary endpoint is overall survival, not an imaging endpoint an open-label investigator could nudge. Twenty-five percent versus zero percent progression-free at twelve months in a randomized comparison is hard to manufacture by chance.

On safety, I would push back on the branding. The name of the program is PRESERVE and the pitch is a wider therapeutic index. Grade three or higher treatment-related adverse events were forty-four point four percent on gotistobart against forty-eight point eight percent on docetaxel. That is parity with one of the more unpleasant chemotherapies we give, not superiority. Grade three or higher colitis was eight point nine percent. And twenty-two point two percent of gotistobart patients discontinued for adverse events against four point nine percent on docetaxel — four and a half times the rate. Preserving peripheral C-T-L-A-four bought a workable drug, not a clean one. Worth adding that the three milligram per kilogram arm was killed by the data monitoring committee after ten patients because outcomes were poor, so there is no room to dose down.

Why this should matter to anyone building depleters rather than treating lung cancer. The transferable idea is that for an antibody whose job is to kill the cell it binds, receptor recycling is a rate-limiting variable and most of us treat it as an afterthought behind affinity and Fc engineering. If your antibody drags its own antigen into the lysosome, you have lowered the surface density you need in order to kill, and you have done it fastest on the cells that express the most. That is precisely backwards from A-D-C design, where internalization is the entire point and you select for antibodies that traffic hard. Same molecular format, opposite requirement, and the switch between those two regimes is a couple of histidines in a C-D-R.

What to watch: stage two is the pivotal one, it is powered on overall survival, it is squamous-only, and it is enrolling at more than a hundred sixty sites. That is the only readout that settles this. And note the company it keeps — a month ago on this show we covered volrustomig, a P-D-one by C-T-L-A-four bispecific, being discontinued in non-small cell lung cancer. The C-T-L-A-four axis is not short of failures. Eighteen point five months is a genuinely arresting number. It is also eighty-seven patients in a dose-finding cohort, and everyone reporting it today knows that.
