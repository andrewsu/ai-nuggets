# Notch1 and Notch2 — more TCF-1-positive progenitors, zero response to PD-L1 blockade, and what that does to your favorite surrogate

Paper link: https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.09.01.748651v1

## Script

Today is Labor Day, so the trade press is a wasteland — Endpoints has one story up, a Novo Nordisk cardiovascular futility call, Fierce is serving me a four-oh-three, and everything else in the window is Friday's news I've already covered or share buybacks and Nasdaq inducement grants. So it's a preprint day. The good news is that the preprint I want to talk about is one you should read anyway, and it goes directly at something I said on this show eight days ago.

It's out of Nathalie Labrecque's lab at the I-R-C-M in Montreal, with Ivan Maillard at Penn and Christian Siebel at Genentech — Siebel matters here because the Delta-like blocking antibodies are his. Labrecque's group has spent a decade on Notch signaling in acute C-D-eight responses, where Notch drives short-lived effector differentiation. The question they asked this time is what Notch does during chronic antigen, and the answer is not the one you'd guess from the acute work.

The tool is clean. They crossed Notch-one and Notch-two floxed mice to E-eight-I-cre, so both receptors are deleted only in mature peripheral C-D-eight T cells — not in the thymus, not in C-D-four cells, not anywhere else. Then chronic L-C-M-V, clone thirteen. And at day thirty, the Notch-deficient mice have more antigen-specific C-D-eight T cells than controls, not fewer. More cells. But those cells wear higher P-D-one, TIM-three, C-D-one-sixty and L-A-G-three, they co-express more inhibitory receptors per cell, they make less interferon-gamma, less T-N-F and less granzyme B on peptide restimulation, and they express more TOX, the master exhaustion transcription factor. Viral load is higher in blood, spleen and brain. And to rule out the obvious circularity — that the cells look worse because the virus is worse — they transferred P-fourteen T-C-R-transgenic cells of both genotypes into the same recipients. Matched viral load, and only the Notch-deficient cells were severely exhausted. So it's cell-intrinsic.

Now the result that made me want to do this episode. They treated infected mice with anti-P-D-L-one every three days from day twenty-three to day thirty-five and read out at day thirty-seven. In control mice it works the way it's supposed to: interferon-gamma production goes up, blood viral load comes down. In the Notch-deficient mice, nothing. No functional rescue, no reduction in viral load.

Here's why that should bother you. Those same Notch-deficient mice have *more* T-C-F-one-positive progenitors than the controls. Substantially more. The whole framework the field runs on says checkpoint blockade acts on the T-C-F-one-positive, S-L-A-M-F-six-positive progenitor pool, and therefore that pool's size is a reasonable surrogate for whether a response is salvageable. This paper has an animal with an inflated progenitor pool and a completely dead response to P-D-L-one blockade. The progenitor count and the response came apart.

What's missing is the next step. They profiled sorted antigen-specific cells at day eight and day twenty-one with single-nucleus R-N-A and A-T-A-C multiome, and the day-twenty-one proportions are stark. Exhausted progenitors are thirty-four percent of the Notch-deficient cells versus eight-point-two percent of controls. Effector-like exhausted cells — the C-X-three-C-R-one-positive population — are twenty-eight percent of controls and two percent of the knockout. Terminally exhausted cells are north of thirty percent of the knockout and one percent of controls. And there's a granzyme-A-high terminal population that's thirty-plus percent of controls and under one percent of the knockout, which flow cytometry couldn't see. So the terminal compartment isn't just bigger without Notch, it's a different and worse terminal compartment.

Pseudotime says control progenitors pass through a type-one-interferon-responsive intermediate and then branch — either to effector-like, or to that granzyme-A-high terminal state. Without Notch, the same intermediate feeds a single line straight to terminal exhaustion. That's a pseudotime claim, so I'd normally discount it, except they went and did the transfer. They sorted progenitor cells from both genotypes at day twenty-one and put them into separately infected matched recipients. Seven days later the control progenitors had become effector-like — S-L-A-M-F-six down, C-X-three-C-R-one up — and the Notch-deficient progenitors had made significantly more C-D-one-oh-one-positive terminal cells. That's the experiment that carries the paper. The progenitor is not the destination; the transition out of it is a licensed step, and Notch is part of the license.

The mechanism they land on is, of all things, geography. Without Notch, chromatin accessibility at K-L-F-two-associated motifs drops and K-L-F-two transcription falls. K-L-F-two is what lets these cells leave secondary lymphoid organs — and sure enough, there are far fewer antigen-specific C-D-eight cells in the blood at day thirty. So the cells get stuck in the lymph node and spleen, and while they're stuck they keep eating signal: A-P-one and BATF motifs open up, Junb, Nfat5 and the whole N-R-four-A family go up, all of which read as stronger and more sustained T-C-R signaling. On top of that, Smad-two-Smad-three motifs are enriched and integrin beta-one is the single most differentially expressed gene, which is the T-G-F-beta retention program. There's also a cytokine-sensing defect — the top upstream regulators in the knockout are cytokines and STATs including I-L-twenty-one and I-L-two, and the C-D-four-unhelped signature is enriched, even though I-L-twenty-one receptor transcription doesn't move. As for where the Notch signal comes from, deleting Delta-like one and four specifically on fibroblastic reticular cells partially phenocopies it — the progenitor accumulation reproduces, the rest is milder — so stroma is a real source but not the only one.

Now the caveats, and there are some that matter. First, this is deletion from the moment of activation onward. The experiment that tells you whether any of this is druggable is the timed antibody blockade, and the anti-Delta-like-one-and-four blocking work is summarized in the discussion — they say early blockade matters and late blockade can't rescue — without a figure callout you can check it against. That's the most translationally load-bearing experiment in the paper and it's the one presented as a sentence. Second, the type-one-interferon arm of the model is motif-and-signature level. An anti-interferon-alpha-receptor blocking protocol is written out in the methods, dose by dose, and no result from it appears in the results, the discussion or any figure legend — and the discussion still lists interferon exposure versus interferon sensitivity as an open question for future work. Third, the anti-P-D-L-one experiment is two independent experiments, and the subset-redistribution panel is one of two. Fourth, and biggest for you: there is no tumor here. Not one. It's L-C-M-V start to finish.

So what do you do with it. The authors' own stated implication is the symmetric one — inhibit Notch to bank progenitors during adoptive cell therapy, provide Notch when you want effectors — and that's fine as far as it goes, but I think their data argue something sharper and less comfortable. If you are characterizing a C-A-R T product or a T-cell-engager-driven response and you are reporting the T-C-F-one-positive stem-like fraction as evidence of durability, this paper is a counterexample sitting right there: a population with four times the progenitor frequency that cannot complete the transition and cannot be rescued by checkpoint blockade. Progenitor frequency without evidence of transition competence is not a durability readout. It might be the opposite — a pile-up.

Read it against the Roche preprint I covered on the thirtieth, the P-D-one I-L-two-v paper that argued the effector-versus-exhaustion branch gets decided in the draining lymph node before the cell ever reaches the tumor. That one identified where the fork is. This one names an input at the same fork — stromal Delta-like ligands — and a chromatin gate, K-L-F-two accessibility, and then shows the fork can be jammed in a way that inflates the upstream pool. Two independent groups, two different systems, converging on the lymph node as the place where the decision that matters actually gets made. And the K-L-F-two-egress mechanism has an uncomfortable corollary for cell therapy: how long your cells linger in lymphoid tissue may not be a neutral engineering parameter, because dwell time is dose — of T-C-R signal, and of T-G-F-beta.
