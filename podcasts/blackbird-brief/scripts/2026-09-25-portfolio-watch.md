Two trials. The registrational stiff person syndrome study, twenty-six patients, twelve months. And the Phase 2 portion of the registrational myasthenia gravis study, seven patients.

Take the stiff person data first, because it is the one heading for a filing. The primary endpoint is the timed twenty-five-foot walk. Median improvement from baseline was forty-six percent at week sixteen and forty-nine percent at month twelve, with a p-value below zero point zero zero zero one. Ninety-five percent of the patients who had a clinically meaningful improvement at the primary analysis — defined as twenty percent or better — still had it at a year. More than a third of patients now complete the walk in under five seconds. Of the twelve patients who came in needing a walking aid, sixty-seven percent no longer need assistance. Every secondary endpoint held at twelve months. And ninety-two percent of patients remained free of chronic immunotherapy for the disease.

The myasthenia arm is smaller and earlier but points the same way. All seven patients hit clinically meaningful improvement at twenty-four weeks on both of the co-primary scales that the ongoing Phase 3 will be judged on, with mean reductions of eight point three and eleven point seven points. Five patients have now reached a year or beyond, out to a year and a half, and all five have maintained it. Six of the seven are off immunosuppressants.

Now the sentence that matters for us. In both trials: no high-grade cytokine release syndrome, no neurotoxicity, and no cases of immune effector cell hemophagocytic syndrome. Chief executive Warner Biddle attributed that directly to two variables — a construct he called unique, the only C-D nineteen C-A-R in autoimmunity with a fully human C-D twenty-eight co-stimulatory domain designed for safety, and what the company describes as a well-established, validated manufacturing process. The rolling biologics licence application completes in the fourth quarter and they are asking for priority review. If it clears, it is the first C-A-R T-cell therapy approved for an autoimmune disease. The stock went up seven percent premarket and William Blair called the data strong and de-risking.

Three reads, and the first one requires us to change a document.

On September second we wrote a control-architecture row into the Winnow target product profile, on the back of Novartis halting eight trials of rapcabtagene autoleucel after three deaths from that hemophagocytic syndrome and Bristol Myers Squibb voluntarily pausing its own autoimmune C-A-R T program. The argument we made was that expansion is the efficacy engine and the toxicity driver on a single knob — you cannot dial down the expansion causing the hyperinflammation without dialling down the depletion causing the remission — and that a bispecific engager is a dosed drug, so it is titratable, interruptible, discontinuable, and needs no lymphodepletion.

The mechanics of that argument are untouched. An autologous cell product is still not titratable. What Kyverna removed is the empirical premise the argument was leaning on. If the field's answer to the hemophagocytic problem turns out to be "use a different co-stimulatory domain and a slower manufacturing process," then the safety differential we were implicitly claiming shrinks toward zero, and what is left in that row is convenience and cost — no apheresis, no vein-to-vein time, off the shelf — which are real, but which are not the word "safer." Jon, Eddie: rewrite the row before it goes in front of an investor, not after. And write the second half honestly too — the first autoimmune C-A-R T approval is now probably a twenty twenty-seven event, which means Winnow's comparator at launch is an approved product rather than an idea.

Second read, and this one is a gift. The second read cuts against Clasp and it is now a pattern rather than an anecdote.
