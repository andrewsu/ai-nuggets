Not statistically significant. At month thirty-six, against the previous version of that same database, the figure was seventy-five percent. The stock fell about forty percent on the open.

Here is what actually happened. Fifty-three percent of the forty-eight-month reference data in the updated control database was missing, and chief executive Matt Kapusta told investors that the patients who dropped out of the control were declining markedly faster than the ones who stayed — so the comparator reflected a progressively healthier population, a bias that runs against the drug rather than for it. Guggenheim's analysts added that one outlier contributed roughly a third of the degradation between month thirty-six and month forty-eight. Geoff, Hemaka, Emily — on September fourth we logged the external-control-source question as an open item in the Aletira pre-I-N-D ask. This is the worked failure case, and it gives us three specifications rather than a principle. First, an external control is a versioned dataset. uniQure filed against one version and read out against the next, so the ask has to lock the version and the analysis rule, not just name the source. Second, attrition in a progressive-disease cohort is not random — it is informative censoring, and it biases the comparator healthier as follow-up lengthens. For a single-administration therapy whose whole value proposition is durability, that means your apparent effect decays for reasons that have nothing to do with your drug. Third, at twelve patients against a comparator that can move, one person's trajectory moved a third of the point estimate.

The half in our favor is worth as much. Total functional capacity — the cruder measure, the one about whether a person can hold a job — came in at sixty-one percent at forty-eight months against sixty at thirty-six. It replicated, and it is the primary endpoint of uniQure's confirmatory trial. The sensitive composite moved; the functional one did not. Aletira has machine-measured auditory brainstem thresholds and no validated functional scale, and no natural-history cohort of genetically confirmed children exists anywhere. The September fourteenth action was fund the cohort, not a bespoke scale. Yesterday adds the spec: fund it with a pre-agreed version lock and a pre-specified missing-data rule, because a cohort you keep updating is a comparator that can be used against you.
