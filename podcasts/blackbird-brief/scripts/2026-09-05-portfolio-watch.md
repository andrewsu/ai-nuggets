## Script

Good morning, Blackbird team. This is the Blackbird Brief Portfolio Watch for Saturday, September fifth. The most important development for the portfolio is an efficacy headline that is really a design result: AbbVie won the first randomized Phase three monotherapy trial a B-C-M-A T-cell engager has ever won, and the molecule that won it was built by making the C-D three arm deliberately weaker.

The drug is etentamig. The trial is CERVINO, in triple-class-exposed relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma, against standard available therapies rather than placebo. Four hundred twenty-one patients randomized, a median of three prior lines, three hundred ninety-three evaluable at the interim, median follow-up eleven point four months. Both primary endpoints met: overall response rate seventy-four percent against forty-five point seven, and a progression-free survival hazard ratio of zero point four — a sixty percent reduction in the risk of progression or death. The monitoring committee unblinded the study early on the strength of it. Full data goes to a plenary in Glasgow at the end of the month.

The part that matters to us is the safety table. Cytokine release syndrome in twenty-eight point three percent, almost all grade one, and not a single grade three or higher case. Exactly one patient developed neurotoxicity, grade one. Compare the marketed competitors: roughly seventy-three percent cytokine release with Johnson and Johnson's Tecvayli, more than fifty percent with Pfizer's Elrexfio, forty-six percent with Regeneron's Lynozyfic. And etentamig needs one step-up dose before monthly administration, where the others need two and a longer step-down.

That profile is not luck. AbbVie built the molecule with a low-affinity C-D three binding domain specifically to hold cytokine release down, then compensated for the weak T-cell arm with a high-avidity bivalent B-C-M-A domain that grabs the tumor cell twice, and kept Fc receptor recycling intact to buy the monthly schedule. The investigator framing is about access — outpatient and community settings, not specialized centers only.

Jon, Eddie — on the second we opened a row in the Winnow target product profile we called control architecture, and said it needed numbers before an investor asked. Here are numbers. A C-D three engager whose T-cell arm was intentionally de-tuned produced a seventy-four percent response rate with zero severe cytokine release. That is the cleanest available demonstration that in an engager, potency and toxicity are separable knobs — precisely what a living, expanding cell product cannot claim, and the same argument William Blair made from the other direction when Novartis halted eight autoimmune CAR-T trials.

Three limits, in the deck before someone else finds them. The cytokine release comparisons are cross-trial, from a topline release, with denominators not broken out — directional until Glasgow. What etentamig actually differentiates on is administration setting, which is a commercial advantage, not a biological one. And the real one: B-C-M-A sits at very high density on plasma cells, which is what makes the avidity trade work. Buy back C-D three affinity with bivalent target binding and you need enough target.

Avi, that last caveat cuts hardest at Clasp. A T-C-R-mimic engager sees a peptide-H-L-A complex present at a few hundred to a few thousand copies per cell, not the tens of thousands B-C-M-A offers. So this does not transfer to Clasp as a recipe. It transfers as a question: when target density will not support the avidity trick, which knob is left, and what does the development plan say about it? Same conversation as the eligible-fraction arithmetic we have been asking for since the twenty-seventh.

The lead is a selective, biased agonist of the melanocortin-four receptor, oral and once daily, into Phase one by the end of this year in Bardet-Biedl syndrome and hypothalamic obesity.
